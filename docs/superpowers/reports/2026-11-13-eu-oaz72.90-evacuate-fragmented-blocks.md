# eu-oaz72.90: evacuation considers every block, and heavy fragmentation evacuates

Date: 2026-11-13. Bead: eu-oaz72.90 (from eu-oaz72.73, PR #1171).

## Diagnosis

A per-collection log of `228_bc34x_meta_body_memoisation.eu` under
`--heap-limit-mib 12` (temporary instrumentation, not committed) showed
about 7 MiB live in about 578 blocks (18 MiB). About 95% of the blocks were
fragmented (25–75% of lines marked), and every one of them was in `unswept`
after each collection. Two defects kept them there:

1. `analyze_collection_strategy` and `candidate_block_bases` indexed only
   `head` (0), `overflow` (1) and `rest` (2..). Blocks in `unswept` and
   `recycled` were never candidates.
2. With 30% or more of blocks fragmented the strategy was
   `DefragmentationSweep`, whose `candidates()` was empty ("TODO: all
   blocks"). A heavily fragmented heap never evacuated at all.

Fault injection shows defect 2 decides the harness outcome: with only
defect 1 reintroduced, `test-compaction` still passes at 9–12 MiB, because
enough fragmented blocks reach `rest` through `head`. Defect 1 is gated by
unit tests.

## Change (`src/eval/memory/heap.rs`)

- `HeapState::indexed_bump_blocks` and `bump_block_at` enumerate `head`,
  `overflow`, then `rest`, `unswept` and `recycled`. Analysis, stress mode and
  `candidate_block_bases` share the enumeration, so indices agree. Existing
  `rest` indices are unchanged.
- `DefragmentationSweep` is removed. Under 10% fragmented blocks: mark in
  place. From 30%: mark in place unless the blocks held pass 3/4 of the heap
  limit (`footprint_under_pressure`). Otherwise: evacuate.
- Candidates are the unpinned sparse and fragmented blocks, sparsest first,
  while their marked lines fit `evacuation_budget_lines` (an eighth of the
  heap's lines, at least four blocks' worth).

Why the pressure gate: an evacuating collection records and updates every
live object, however few blocks it moves. Without the gate, evacuating heavy
fragmentation under the default limit raised 022_hof_fold's mark time from
0.64 s to 0.79–0.93 s (measured-single), whatever the budget, even at four
blocks. With the gate it is 0.58 s.

## Known limitation (follow-up)

The mark phase line-marks an object's old copy before evacuating it, so an
evacuated block frees only at the next collection. `EU_GC_STRESS=1` now
evacuates every block in every list, so under a tight limit it holds about
twice the live data. `test-compaction` fails at 10–16 MiB under stress, and
228 under stress at 14–16 MiB (it passes at 20). Master passes 228 there,
because its stress mode reached only `rest`. The default 32 GiB limit, which
the CI stress jobs use, is unaffected. Freeing evacuated lines in the same
collection would need care: a non-evacuable backing buffer can stay behind in
a candidate block.
