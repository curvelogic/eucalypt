# eu-oaz72.4 — GC volume trigger: design and measurements

Date: 2026-11-12. Bead: eu-oaz72.4. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-aoc-2026-preparation.md`.

## Problem

`Heap::policy_requires_collection` returned true only when allocated blocks
reached the heap limit (and a cumulative recycled/allocated ratio was below
0.25). Under the 32 GiB default the collector never ran; under a small
`--heap-limit-mib` it ran on nearly every 500-step poll once at the limit and
the heap kept growing past it (the limit was never enforced on allocation).

## Policy

- **Volume.** `HeapState::lines_since_gc` counts lines handed to the
  allocator: a fresh block's 256, a recycled block's free lines (counting it
  whole over-counted badly in mostly-live heaps: 46 collections instead of 21
  on the 100k-element sum below), and each large object's size. Reset at
  every collection.
- **Live.** Bytes of every object at its *first* mark
  (`CollectorHeapView::mark`, `mark_raw_bytes`, `mark_array`). Line marks
  were rejected as the measure: an evacuated copy re-marks its lines while
  the old copy's stay marked, double-counting under evacuation.
- **Trigger.** `max(32 MiB, growth × live)`; `growth` starts at 4, doubles
  (cap 8) after a collection reclaiming < 1/4 of what was allocated, and
  resets after one reclaiming > 1/2. Factor 2 cost 1.6× wall on
  `022_hof_fold` (13 collections); 4 gives 7.
- **Limit.** Trigger capped at the headroom below the limit; live > 7/8 of
  the limit after a collection raises `ExecutionError::HeapLimitExceeded`.

## Marking costs the old policy hid

Collecting regularly exposed two O(blocks)-per-object searches:

1. `HeapState::mark_region_in_block` searched `rest`/`unswept`/`recycled`
   linearly for the block of every marked object. Now a base-address index of
   `Weak<RefCell<LineMap>>` (line maps are `Rc`-shared by `BumpBlock`), with a
   multiplicative hasher; a stale entry for a dropped block fails to upgrade
   and falls back to the search, so no dangling access is possible.
2. `is_in_candidate_block` scanned the candidate list per object; the
   candidates are now resolved to a base-address set once per collection.

On `range(0,100000)` summed and counted (stack-retained, ~240 MB live), mark
time went 92 s → 29 s (index) → 5.5 s (candidate set) → 3.1 s (free-line
volume) → 0.6 s (growth 4). *measured-single*.

## Acceptance status

Policy implemented; the repro meets the bead's criteria. The bead's
acceptance measurement on the AoC 2025 days is pending eu-oaz72.34 and
eu-oaz72.27 (plan §3), so the bead stays open.

## Known residue

- Large-object blocks are never freed (`HeapState::lobs` only grows);
  their size counts as allocation and, when marked, as live.
- With collections now running in the harness, `EU_GC_VERIFY`/`EU_GC_POISON`
  print eu-y6fx's large-object `mark_region_in_block` diagnostic and the
  heuristic `GC HOLE WARNING` (it matches headers marked in *either* state, so
  a dead object marked in an earlier cycle trips it). Neither failed a test.
